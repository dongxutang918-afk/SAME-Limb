- **HUMIT Lab white wordmark:** `humit-lab-wordmark-original.png` (425 × 142 px, PNG with alpha). This official variant reads “HUMIT Lab” with the red U and the Chinese laboratory name in white. Use on a dark background and preserve its aspect ratio.

- **HUMIT Lab illustrated logo:** `humit-lab-logo-original.png` (425 × 118 px, PNG with alpha). This is the official lab navigation logo, containing the blue/yellow symbol, red U, and dark lettering. Its dark lettering needs a light background.
