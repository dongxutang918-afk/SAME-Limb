MULTIMODAL LOWER-LIMB DATASET

SAME-Limb

Synchronized AMG and EMG Dataset of Lower-limb Muscle Activities in Everyday Training

A synchronized multimodal lower-limb dataset integrating AMG, EMG, and optical motion-capture measurements across everyday training activities and walking conditions.
